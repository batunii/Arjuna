## Read

1. **Focus benefit is near-unanimous (19/20).** Every participant endorsed easier attention,
   obvious focus location, and reduced bother from edge events, and denied looking away as
   often. The one dissent is P4 — the strongest behavioural performer (78% baseline) — who
   says it made "no real difference" to concentration while endorsing the other four items.
2. **Comfort is a non-issue at the current dim (15% periphery luminance).** 4/4: boundary
   not distracting in itself (the 24° soft edge doing its job); 4/4: no eye strain; 3/4:
   effect faded into the background. P3 alone found it uncomfortable to look at (also the
   behaviourally disengaged participant).
3. **The headline: 4/4 "noticed side events later" — a finding with two readings, and the
   questionnaire alone cannot split them (user point, 2026-07-23).**
   *Cost reading* (how section B is keyed, the H2b analogue): the filter delays peripheral
   awareness — bad wherever peripheral events are time-critical. *Mechanism reading*: this
   is the intended behaviour experienced from the inside — peripheral onsets no longer
   CAPTURE attention immediately; events that persist get attended eventually (deferred,
   not denied). Under this reading B3=4/4 is closer to a subjective manipulation check
   PASSING than a cost — "I noticed everything instantly, same as always" would suggest
   the manipulation wasn't doing anything.
   The behaviour favours the mechanism reading *for task-relevant events*: detection
   unchanged-to-better, RTs faster — the felt delay never materialised against anything
   we asked them to catch. The unmeasured gap is task-IRRELEVANT events, exactly where
   the readings diverge — and John's Point 2 decoy-target suggestion is the instrument
   that splits them (decoys in unimportant regions: slower/fewer responses under filter =
   desired filtering; delayed responses to meaningful-but-unexpected events = the hazard).
   Either way the acceptance observation stands: 3/4 uncomfortable not seeing everything,
   2/4 worried, 2/4 uneasy — **the perceived cost exceeds the measured cost**, so trust in
   the periphery is a design target of its own regardless of which reading is true.
4. **Internal consistency caveat**: binary administration loses the graded signal the
   instrument was designed for (medians/IQRs, α); P4's A1-vs-A4 tension suggests at least
   one acquiescent or ambivalent response. Return to the 7-point scale for the formal runs.
