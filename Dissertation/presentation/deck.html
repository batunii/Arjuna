01 · Framing

One question, two halves

Can subtractive attention guidance be delivered on consumer XR hardware — and when it is delivered, does it actually help human attention?

Part T — “Can it be built?”

What degree of diminishment control is achievable on a consumer VST headset without OS-level access to the passthrough layer — and at what cost to image quality, FOV, latency, comfort?

Answered by: design space (Ch. 3) → reference implementation (Ch. 4) → falsifiable benchmarks (Ch. 5)

Part H — “Does it work?”

Under controlled distraction, does peripheral diminishment reduce the cost distractors impose on a focal task — without unacceptably degrading peripheral awareness?

Answered by: pre-registered within-subjects study, N = 20 (Ch. 6) — the only confirmatory claims made

Neither half stands alone: Part T without Part H is an engineering report; Part H without Part T is a psychology study run on a video player. The coupling is deliberate and stated up front.

01 · Framing

Motivation: attention is the scarce resource — and the field's answer has been additive

The quantified problem

Task-irrelevant peripheral events capture attention involuntarily; each capture carries a performance penalty (Itti, Koch & Niebur 1998).

2.4×
commission errors under peripheral distractors in HMD sustained attention:
1.33 → 3.15, p < .001, N = 66

Frontiers in Human Neuroscience (2025), virtual-classroom Go/No-go — the effect this dissertation's primary hypothesis is built on top of.

Additive → Subtractive

  • Convention: add arrows, halos, attention funnels (Biocca et al. 2006)
  • This work: subtract — dim, mute, re-grade the periphery so it pulls less
  • = Diminished Reality (Mori et al. 2017; Cheng et al. 2022); recently framed as “visual noise cancellation” (2024)

Why VST, why now

A VST headset re-displays the world on an opaque screen — in principle every pixel is modifiable, unlike additive OST optics. Quest 3 makes this consumer-scale. In principle.

Backing: Itti/Koch/Niebur TPAMI 1998 · Frontiers Hum. Neurosci. 2025 (N=66) · Biocca CHI 2006 · Mori et al. 2017 · Cheng et al. CHI 2022 · ACM 3634699 (visual noise cancellation).
01 · Framing

The motivating technical problem: the compositing constraint

What the OS forbids

  • Passthrough is composited by the OS: apps cannot read, shade, or spatially mask it
  • Apps may only draw on top, or apply a global colour transform
  • “Sample the pixel, darken it, write it back” is architecturally impossible — a deliberate privacy/safety boundary that strengthens over time

What the camera API grants (PCA, 2025)

  • Raw forward feed as GPU texture + intrinsics + pose
  • But: mono 1280×960, ~85–90° FOV vs the native ~110° stereo view
  • Full pixel control exists only where image quality is worst

Subtractive guidance on consumer hardware is therefore a compositing-rights problem — “dim everything except what matters, without degrading what matters” cannot be done by editing the image, because no app is permitted to edit it.

Backing: Meta PCA docs 2025 · no VST headset reaches normal acuity, Quest 3 worse in low light (arXiv 2601.02805) · Vision Pro: camera enterprise-only, dimming global (Apple 2024) · OST cannot subtract light at all (Itoh et al. 2021).

03 · Design Space

The walls are findings too

No read access

At any tier, to the pixels the user actually sees. Even Tier 3 supplies a different, lower-grade copy of reality. Content-adaptive effects can only respond to the proxy.

No spatial modulation of the native layer

Full quality can be revealed or hidden — never partially processed. “Blur the real periphery”, the gentlest DR primitive (Patney 2016), cannot exist here except via the Tier-3 copy.

No subtraction on OST

Additive optics cannot remove photons. The entire Tier-2 family is VST-specific — stated before an examiner asks (slide 27).

Why bet on the walls staying up

Read access = privacy exposure; write access = safety exposure. Both concerns strengthen over time, so research assuming future read–modify–write access bets against platform vendors' incentives. This work treats the constraint as permanent — which is what makes the design space durable: any future platform is located in it by asking which wall it removes.

Source: ch3 §3.2, §3.4 · precedent for wall-hardening: surface-projected passthrough deprecated mid-project at SDK v83 (failure museum #2).
03 · Design Space → 04 · System

The load-bearing insight: the focus window is an absence, not a rendering

At Tier 2, the highest-fidelity rendering of reality is an absence of rendering.
  • Inside the window: alpha = 0, nothing drawn → the user sees native OS passthrough at full quality, full ~110° FOV, with the OS's own latency compensation
  • The manipulation lives only in the periphery, where human acuity is lowest and precision matters least
  • Inverts the naive design (re-render everything, accept camera-grade quality everywhere)

One line of shader arithmetic…

alpha = t  (t = smoothstep window coordinate)

…simultaneously solves the FOV problem (camera covers only ~85–90°) and the fidelity problem (mono 1280×960 vs native stereo) — by ensuring the reconstruction is only shown where neither limitation matters.

Why it matters for the study

The system escapes the passthrough-acuity ceiling exactly where the participant is asked to look — no current VST headset reaches normal acuity (arXiv 2601.02805), so putting the task region on the best path is a validity decision, not aesthetics.

Backing: ch3 §3.3.2, ch4 §4.2.3 · punch-through mechanism is documented platform practice (Meta Passthrough Windows; Immersed Portals 2022) — but all prior uses reveal passthrough surrounded by virtual content; as the focus region of a diminished periphery: unprecedented (prior-art search).

Why this is a result, not a convenience

Every tier of the taxonomy is reachable from one fragment program: alpha 0 = Tier-2 absence · alpha 1 black = Tier-2 blackout · alpha 1 camera-fed = Tier-3 re-render. Adding a mode = adding a per-fragment policy, which is why ten modes could be designed and compared cheaply.

World-locked by construction

Window stored in world spherical coordinates, not screen/head space → it stays fixed to the desk or doorway as the head turns. Manager feeds the stateless shader through uniforms; single-pass instanced stereo with no eye-dependent sampling anywhere. Projection derived from PCA intrinsics (res / 2f) — which removed the early zoom mismatch against the native window.

04 · System (technical contribution)

Making a mono camera binocular: world-direction fusion sampling

The failure (measured, on device)

First shader sampled the camera texture by screen position. The same world point projects to different screen positions per eye → each eye received a different camera pixel for the same point → frank double vision. Code “correct” per eye, broken as a binocular system.

Lesson: in stereo rendering, eye-independence of the sampling domain is a correctness criterion, not an optimisation.

The fix

Sample by world direction through a head-centred pinhole: project each fragment's direction onto the camera basis, divide by intrinsics-derived tan-half-FOV. World direction is eye-independent → both eyes sample the same pixel → the mono image reads as a texture painted on the world shell, and the visual system fuses it.

uv = ((dir·rt, dir·up) / dir·fwd) / tanHalfFov · 0.5 + 0.5

Dual cameras: hard split by in-view weight, never a crossfade — crossfading two offset viewpoints reproduces exactly the ghosting this scheme exists to avoid.

Backing: ch4 §4.4, failure museum #1 · comfort constraint on record: a mono feed must never be presented as geometry-correct stereo (memory: PCA mono-camera stereo comfort).

Deployable today

Early-return shader path: no texture reads, no camera permission, negligible GPU, no thermal ceiling — the only modes plausibly wearable for hour-long sessions on present hardware (published Tier-3 systems throttle in 5–10 min).

04 · System

ColorPop: the literature's strongest guidance channels, as a standing property of the visual field

Four-level salience hierarchy (two tracks × window)

outside-other < outside-ROG < inside-other < inside-ROG

  • ROG classification: red/orange/green hue bands gated by saturation floors — warm floor 0.35 keeps warm-white headlights out of the “orange” class while real lamps (sat > 0.5) stay in
  • Inside-ROG: saturation ×1.7 · Sutton et al.'s sigmoid contrast recipe (α=10, β=0.5, strength 0.6) · brightness ×1.15
  • Outside-ROG: natural colour, dimmed ×0.8 — a peripheral traffic light must never become invisible

Two safeguards + the luminance win

  • Glare compression: bright + unsaturated + non-ROG (= headlight glare) compressed toward a visible dim spot, never black (0.35 inside / 0.85 outside)
  • Blown-core guard: Gaussian surround check exempts the clipped white centre of a saturated signal lamp from the glare mask
  • Global periphery dim ×0.85: the window also wins on plain luminance — the strongest single channel in the attention literature. Net periphery ≈ 0.68 of natural
  • Colour/grey boundaries read harsher than darkness → soft edge widened 20° → 32°

Detector-free by design: keys on luminance and colour only — no object detection anywhere in its pipeline. That single decision makes the study's findings independent of any perception stack (slide 19).

Backing: luminance/saturation as channels: Bailey 2009 · Grogorick 2017 · Sutton UIST 2022 (recipe implemented as published) · ch4 §4.5.3 — all values are named shader parameters, checkable in-repo.
04 · System

Comfort and interaction: policies, not tunings

Motion suppression — Norouzi (2018), inverted

Head-coupled vignetting increases sickness → so this system decouples the opposite way: effect fades out during fast head motion, returns on settle.

ModeYield thresholdHold
ColorPop35°/s0.7 s
Soft Dark50°/s1.0 s
Hard Dark70°/s1.5 s
  • Thresholds scale with intrusiveness: wrongly un-suppressing a blackout is jarring; a gentle mode costs nothing
  • Asymmetric fades — yield in 0.20 s (must feel instant to be trusted), return over 0.70 s
  • Looking back into the window (+15° margin) restores immediately; suppression multiplies, never resets, formation — user-created state is veiled, never destroyed

Interaction rationales (each explicit)

  • Painting, not pointing — a focus region is a region; sweeping makes its extent a deliberate act instead of hiding it in an auto-size heuristic
  • World-locked, not head-locked — the window belongs to the task surface; a head-locked window would chase attention rather than anchor it (and head-coupling is the comfort hazard above)
  • Minimal feedback — corner dots auto-hide after 3 s; mode toast fades; controller-only input (removes an input-ambiguity class from study sessions)
  • Every temporal parameter Inspector-tunable — because DR transition dynamics are an identified literature gap, not settled science
Backing: Norouzi, Bruder & Welch, ACM SAP 2018 · ch4 §4.6–4.7 · gap 3 (ch2 §2.8).

06 · User Study (Contribution C4)

Study design: every choice has a citation or a number

Reframed to be falsifiable

Not “does the vignette make people better?” but: distractors impose a known cost (errors 1.33 → 3.15, N=66) — does the vignette reduce that cost? Every outcome is informative; the one failure mode (distractors fail to distract) is gated twice (pilot G1 + in-study check MC1).

Why within-subjects, N = 20 (24 recruited)

  • Precedent: Cheng 2022 ran N=16 and N=12; McLaughlin 2025 within-subject with mixed models
  • Field norms: modal CHI sample is 12; power comes from repeated measures (Caine 2016)
  • Arithmetic: ~140 trials × 4 conditions × 20 ≈ ~11,000 Block A trials for trial-level mixed models

Controls in one line each

  • Vignette isolated as sole IV — window painted & locked even in Off conditions (identical motor/procedural experience)
  • Balanced 4×4 Latin square (A) · parity counterbalance (B) · 3×3 Latin square (C)
  • Practice-to-criterion tutorial includes the vignette → first exposure never lands in a measured trial (novelty control)
  • Neutral “comparing display modes” framing vs demand characteristics
  • Bright constant lighting — passthrough acuity degrades in low light (controlled variable, not convenience)

Ethics & safety

Approved protocol (delta table for changes) · seated, no locomotion, < 45 min headset time · written stop rule applied without discussion · VRSQ pre and post — post-only sickness scores are uninterpretable without baseline.

Backing: ch6 §6.1–6.3 · Caine CHI 2016 · Cheng CHI 2022 · McLaughlin HF 2025 · Kim et al. 2018 (VRSQ) · Frontiers VR 2022 (baseline critique) · testing-strategy-v2.md remains normative.
06 · User Study

Three blocks bracket the feasibility spectrum — mode paired to the paradigm that can detect its mechanism

A · Workstation primary

  • Hard Dark on real passthrough, physical tablet distractors at ±35° — the UFOV zone of maximal involuntary capture
  • 2×2 vignette × distractor, ~3.5 min each; Go/No-go panel or large-print cancellation (pilot gate G2 decides)
  • Dark modes remove signal → paradigm measures what removal buys: reduced distractor cost
  • The real artifact, no simulation, no oracle

B · Driving video secondary

  • ColorPop on the video sphere — simulation-based evaluation in the Cheng (2022) tradition: identical, repeatable, event-dense stimuli
  • ISO 17488 DRT: 24 machine-timed probes/condition — 12 central (<10°), 12 peripheral (25–40°); millisecond logging, no video coding
  • Re-grading → paradigm measures faster focal detection (H2a) and its cost (H2b)

C · Mode sampler exploratory

  • All three modes, 75 s each, common stimulus; Likert + interview (Cheng's pattern)
  • Soft Dark's only user data — a full block doesn't fit the session budget; stated trade-off
  • Skippable by pre-registered rule; confirmatory blocks never compressed to save it

The bridge: if A (real hardware) and B (simulation) agree in direction, the simulation methodology of Cheng/McLaughlin gains credibility; if they diverge, the divergence is itself a reportable finding. Does not transfer (stated before the examiner asks)

  • Attention findings do not transfer automatically from monoscopic video simulation (peripheral motion-in-depth may capture differently) — flagged, not hidden

Transfers

  • The taxonomy — any platform (OST included) is located by which walls it removes; OST's Tier 2 is empty for subtraction, collapsing to Sutton-style additive modulation + dimming hardware
  • The human-factors findings — what peripheral diminishment does to attention is a property of the visual system, not of the display implementing it
  • Comfort findings — as a lower bound only (2026 headset weight is conservative for comfort, orthogonal to attention)

The engineering is platform-bound; the psychology is not. That division is itself a use of the design space.
