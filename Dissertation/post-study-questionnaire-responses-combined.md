# Post-study questionnaire (ESQ) — combined responses

Participant ID / question / answer, compiled from the 20 completed 
`post-study-questionnaire-*.docx` forms in `Dissertation/`. Answers were recorded 
in the third column of each section table as Y/N, normalised here to Yes/No.

Only answered items appear. Every form answered the same 14 items (A1-A5, B1-B5, C1, C2, C4, C5); item C3, all of Section D (D1-D6), Section E (E1 ranking, E2 multi-select) and Section F (F1-F4 open questions) were left blank on all 20 forms and are omitted.

Participants: 20 (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 10, 13, 16, 17, 20, 25, 26, 27, 30, 33, 41, 43, 51, 90). Total answers: 280.

Note: the sheet filed as `15-16` is recorded here under participant 16.
